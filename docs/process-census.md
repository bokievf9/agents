# Process census of HQ (drill 2026-11-21)

Every process of the office that can run, who triggers it, what it must do and how the drill checked it. Written after the drill of 2026-11-21
("проверить, работает ли каждый процесс в офисе"): the suite is `apps/hq/tests/drill/*.test.ts` (run with `pnpm --filter hq test`), the world it uses
(`tests/drill/world.ts`) wires every head, the Director, the Drill, the admin API, the real webhook server and the real bot glue (`src/glue.ts`) on in-memory
databases with `MemoryMessenger`, `MockLlm` and fake web tools. Status: **pass** (drilled, nothing wrong), **fail, fixed** (the drill found a bug, fixed in this branch with a test that fails without the fix),
**untested** (reason in the note). Tests named without a path are in `apps/hq/tests/drill/`.

Totals: **218 rows**: 189 pass, 24 fail then fixed, 0 fail left, 5 untested.
By section: commands 45, free text 12, approval kinds 21, routines and workers 33, admin routes 65, public routes 12, buttons 17, cross-cutting 13.

Bugs the drill found (all fixed, one commit each, each with a regression test that fails without the fix):
1. A card with a `[цена]`/`[решение владельца]` template: «Одобрить» ran the kind, threw and left the proposal `failed` for good (now held open with the hint, 409 `placeholder` on the site; `KindSpec.ready`).
2. An oversized body: the socket was destroyed before the answer, callers saw a reset instead of the 413.
3. `POST /admin/clients/:id/leads/:leadId/messages` answered 500 `no such table: cs_tickets` on a database where no ticket had ever been made.
4. A level 1 draft with a template acted alone and failed with an error line; it now waits as a card.
5. A card whose first Telegram send failed showed twice in the site inbox and `/approve` sent it a third time.
6. The nightly renders cleanup blocked with `no such table: cm_items` on a database without content tables.
7. Deciding a sibling approval of a blocked task threw `blocked -> done` out of `reconcileApprovals` (button «Не получилось», site 500) after the approval had run.
8. `/help` said 19 executors, `/workers` listed 20.
9. The card of an invoice voided on the site still sent its payment text when approved; now held (`not_ready`).

Not covered by the drill: see the `untested` rows (Instagram Graph and YouTube upload calls, `yt-auth`, the real Telegram network, the heartbeat file) and the notes on partial rows.

AI agents reach small business

Small companies in Central Asia are adopting AI assistants faster than expected, according to a survey published this week by a regional business association.

Most of them start with a chatbot that answers routine questions on Telegram and books appointments, then add lead qualification and invoicing & payments.

“The bot paid for itself in a month,” said the owner of a dental clinic in Almaty.

Analysts warn that quality varies and recommend starting with a narrow task.
